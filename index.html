Why a coalition

Young people are already shaping Nanaimo. We’re here to back them.

When people and programs connect, support becomes easier to find—and harder to fall through.

Growing up should come with people who listen, spaces that feel safe and pathways that feel possible. Yet support can be fragmented, hard to find or out of reach at the moment it matters most.

Nanaimo Youth Coalition is a new table for people who care about young people. We are convening relationships across the community so youth can find belonging, trusted support and meaningful opportunities without having to navigate it all alone.

We are starting small, learning in public and making room for more voices—including the young people whose experience should guide what comes next.
